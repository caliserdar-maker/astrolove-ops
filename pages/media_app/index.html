AstroLoveArt Drive Automation

Internal tool of the AstroLoveArt shop

AstroLoveArt Drive Automation is a private, internal tool used by AstroLoveArt to organize its own design and order files in its own Google Drive.

The tool is used only by the shop owner. It is not offered to the public, it has no sign up, and no other person can use it or connect an account to it.

What it does:

  • Copies and organizes AstroLoveArt design files between the shop owner's Google Drive folders and the shop's automated production jobs.
  • Reads and updates the shop owner's own documents and spreadsheets that track production.

It does not access any other person's Google account or data.
