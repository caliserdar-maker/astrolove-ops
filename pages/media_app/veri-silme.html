Data Deletion Instructions

AstroLoveArt publishing tool. Effective date: November 22, 2026.

Who uses this app

This app is an internal tool of the AstroLoveArt shop. It publishes the shop's own posts to the shop's own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook Page, Threads and YouTube). Only the shop's own accounts are connected to it. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app keeps

  • The access credential that each platform issues when the shop's account authorizes the app.
  • The identifiers of posts that the app published for the shop, and the statistics of those posts if the shop reads them.

The app does not collect data about other users, followers or visitors.

How to remove the app and delete its data

The connected account can remove the app at any time in that platform's own settings:

  • Instagram, Facebook and Threads: open the settings menu, find the section for apps and websites (or website permissions) and remove the app.
  • YouTube (Google account): myaccount.google.com/permissions, then remove the app.

After access is removed, the stored credential no longer works. The shop can delete the stored credential and post records on request.

To ask for deletion of any other data related to this app, send a message through our Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/AstroLoveArt
