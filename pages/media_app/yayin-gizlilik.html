Privacy Policy

AstroLoveArt publishing tool. Effective date: November 22, 2026.

Who uses this app

The AstroLoveArt publishing tool is an internal tool of the AstroLoveArt shop. It publishes the shop's own posts (images, videos and captions made by the shop) to the shop's own accounts on Instagram, Facebook (the shop's Page), Threads and YouTube. Only the shop's own accounts are connected to it. It is not offered to the public and no other person can connect an account to it.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the shop's own connected accounts (account name and identifier), needed to publish to them.
  • The identifiers of the posts that the app publishes, and the statistics of those posts (for example views and likes) if the shop reads them.

The app does not read messages, does not collect data about followers or other users, and does not access any account other than the shop's own.

How the data is used and shared

The data is used only to publish the shop's posts and to measure how the shop's own posts perform. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Storage and security

The access credentials that the platforms issue are kept in the shop's private cloud storage and in encrypted secrets of the shop's automation environment. They are used only by the shop's own automated jobs.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload the shop's videos to the shop's own YouTube channel. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google. Access can be removed at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
