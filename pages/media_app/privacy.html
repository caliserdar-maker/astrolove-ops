Privacy Policy

AstroLoveArt Drive Automation. Effective date: October 22, 2026.

Who uses this app

AstroLoveArt Drive Automation is an internal tool of the AstroLoveArt shop. It is authorized by a single Google account, the shop's own account. It is not available to the public and no other Google account can connect to it.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the Google Drive files, documents and spreadsheets of the shop's own Google account. These include AstroLoveArt design files, production records and order files.

Order files can contain information that AstroLoveArt customers gave when they placed an order, such as the names for a personalized design and the shipping details. The shop uses this information only to make and deliver those orders.

How the data is used and shared

  • Files are copied between the shop's Google Drive and the shop's own automated production jobs.
  • Print files and the shipping details of an order are sent to the print partner that produces and ships that order.
  • Product images may be shared by link so they can be shown on the shop's own marketplace and social media pages.

The app does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine learning models.

Storage and security

Files are kept in the shop's Google Drive. The access credential is kept as an encrypted secret in the shop's automation environment and is used only by the shop's own automated jobs.

Limited Use

AstroLoveArt Drive Automation's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The shop's Google account can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Etsy shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/AstroLoveArt
