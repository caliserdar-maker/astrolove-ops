CONTENTS
Three UTF-8 CSV files with the six requested columns, and 119 retained screenshots. No account settings or content were changed.

COVERAGE AND LIMITATIONS
Pinterest: all 24 seeds searched for autocomplete/results and all 24 in Trends. 50 searches including two repair searches. Seven exact seed charts were available; each chart screenshot is included. United States and last 12 months selected. Peak months were visually read from the historical line, excluding forecast. Missing exact matches mean the chart was not available through this search, not zero demand. Related terms were captured when shown; six chart captures had an empty related-terms area.
Two result captures could not be retained: zodiac wall art and zodiac signs as couples. Their chips are marked not shown with a capture-failure explanation. The search cap prevented repeat searches. Autocomplete text for gift for girlfriend and zodiac wall art was recovered from the collection transcript.
TikTok: autocomplete captured for the first six seeds. Others searched for was visible for anniversary gift. The couple gift result triggered a slider CAPTCHA, and collection on TikTok stopped. Remaining 18 seeds were NOT searched.
Creative Center: United States 7-day and 30-day screens were inspected. The accessible Trends page had no hashtag-search control. Requested-tag posts/views could not be collected. The period options shown were 7, 30 and 90 days; 120 days was not shown. No substitute hashtags or guessed metrics were used.
Instagram: all 24 seeds and all 10 requested hashtags searched, plus five repeat captures (39 searches). The seed suggestions shown were account results. Nine exact hashtag post counts were displayed; #astrology showed only different hashtags, so its count is not shown.

HOW TO READ THE CSV
Ranks start at 1 within each seed/data type, in displayed order. Platform UI labels were retained without translating suggestion text. Instagram profile-image boilerplate was removed from account result labels.
rank_or_value contains the displayed metric or rank. Missing values use not shown; text explains whether the field was absent, the page was blocked, or the seed was not collected. Do not interpret these as zero.
For Trends, a yes row records data availability and a YYYY-MM row records the visually read peak month. Related terms belong to Trends on Pinterest and Others searched for on TikTok.
For Creative Center, the time window is written in text to preserve the requested six-column schema.
Instagram counts are display strings, not exact raw counts. Turkish B abbreviates bin (thousand). Plus signs and unusual punctuation (e.g. 4,400.000) are preserved exactly as shown. No conversion to an exact count is made.
The ordinary Pinterest, TikTok and Instagram searches used the signed-in sessions and their existing locale/personalization. United States was explicitly selected only for Pinterest Trends and Creative Center.
date_collected is the date the observation or missing-data status was recorded, not proof an unsearched seed was queried.
