A personalized 1st anniversary gift for your husband or your wife, made from your two zodiac signs, Cancer and Libra. Your names and your special date are added to your own shared symbol, for married couples and for couples celebrating one year together.

Every AstroLoveArt design merges two zodiac signs into one shared symbol. This listing is for the Cancer and Libra symbol shown in the photos.

HOW TO ORDER
1. Choose your style and your format.
2. Fill in the personalization boxes: the name under Cancer, the name under Libra and your special date (your wedding day or the day you met). Example: Cancer: Emma, Libra: James, 14 June 2025.
3. Heading: "Paper Anniversary" for married couples or "Our First Year" for couples who are dating. The card always says "Happy First Anniversary".
4. Optional short line: up to 20 characters of your own to replace "Year One". Leave it empty to keep "Year One".

FREE PREVIEW BEFORE YOU ORDER
Would you like to see your design first? Message us your names, your date and your chosen style, and we will send you a free preview.

AFTER YOU ORDER
We send your final preview in Etsy Messages within 24 hours. As soon as you approve it, we prepare your file or your print.

DELIVERY
- Digital card and digital file: sent in Etsy Messages as soon as you approve your preview.
- Shipped print: made and shipped within 4 to 7 days after your approval, with free shipping to the US, UK and EU. Estimated delivery: US 3 to 8 days, UK and Australia 5 to 10 days, EU 5 to 12 days. For other countries, see the delivery estimate at checkout.

STYLES
Starlit, Timeless, First Page, Layers, Botanical.

FORMATS
- Digital card 5x7: a single-sided flat card sent as a digital file, to print at home or at a print shop. Nothing is shipped.
- Digital file 8x10: an 8x10 inch, 300 dpi JPG for your own printing. Nothing is shipped.
- Shipped print 8x10: printed on premium matte art paper with long-lasting inks and shipped to you by our production partner.

PLEASE NOTE
The gold, relief and paper layer looks are printed visual effects. Prints are flat matte prints, with no foil, no raised layers and no cut paper pieces. Colors can look slightly different on screens and on paper.

WHY PAPER
Paper is the traditional gift for the first wedding anniversary. A print of your own two signs, your names and your date is a paper gift made only for the two of you.

RETURNS
Every item is personalized, so we can't accept returns or exchanges. Digital files can't be refunded once sent. If your print arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace it at no cost.
