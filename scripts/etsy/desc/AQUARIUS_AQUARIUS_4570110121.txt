Vegan certified paper, plastic free packaging

✦ CARE
Handle by the edges. If a rolled print curls, lay it flat under a weight for a day or two before framing. Frame behind glass, away from direct sunlight.

✦ GOOD TO KNOW
Frames in the photos are for display only and are not included.
Colors may vary slightly between your screen and the printed piece.
On matte cotton paper, deep blacks print as a rich charcoal rather than screen black. This is the natural character of fine art paper.
Every print is made just for you, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges. If yours arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we will reprint it at no cost.
